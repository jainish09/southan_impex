ABOUT SOUTHERN IMPEX

Leading Signage Material Wholesaler Since 2007

Founded with a commitment to support advertising agencies, commercial printers, and architectural fabricators, Southern Impex operates as South India's premier wholesale master supply house for high-grade signage raw materials.

We bridge certified global material manufacturers with regional business requirements. From weather-resistant Flex Banners and high-tack Vinyls to ASTRYX Cast Acrylic, ACP Facade Panels, and Samsung LED modules, our inventory guarantees consistent specifications and long-term durability.
